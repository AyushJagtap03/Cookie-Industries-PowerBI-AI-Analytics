# Project Details — Cookie Industries Dashboard

## Overview
An interactive Power BI report exploring 700 sales transaction records to understand sales performance, product-level profitability, time trends, and geographic performance.

## Report questions
- What are the headline sales and profitability KPIs?
- Which products lead by sales and profitability?
- How does performance vary month to month and by day of week?
- How do sales differ by country and region?

## Dashboard components
- Headline KPI cards: gross sales, units sold, sales records, average sales per record, and net profit margin.
- Supporting KPI indicators: total net profit and best-selling product.
- Monthly units-sold trend and monthly sales chart.
- Product profitability comparison.
- Sales by region and country.
- Sales by day of week.
- Slicers for month, year, country, region, and product.

## Tools used
- **Microsoft Power BI:** interactive report and visual design.
- **Power Query:** data shaping and preparation.
- **DAX:** measures and calculations.
- **ChatGPT:** AI-assisted troubleshooting, formula support, problem-solving, and iterative visual/report refinement.

AI was used as a development assistant. The project is not represented as a deployed AI model or fully automated AI system.

## How to view
Download the PBIX file from this repository and open it in Microsoft Power BI Desktop. Use the slicers to explore the data.

## Notes
Metrics reflect the source dataset and the filter selection in the report. Please confirm currency and unit formatting from the source data before interpreting values externally.
